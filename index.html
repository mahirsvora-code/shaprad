Our Heritage

Shaprad Boutique
Ambawadi, Ahmedabad

Shaprad Boutique is a premium fashion destination in Ambawadi, Ahmedabad, renowned since 1997 for bespoke made-to-measure and curated ready-to-wear collections.

Specialising in custom-stitched salwar suits, designer blouses, lehenga cholis, and western wear, Shaprad blends timeless craftsmanship with modern design.

With a legacy of exclusivity, flawless fittings, and personalised service, Shaprad Boutique remains the preferred choice for women seeking elegance and individuality in every outfit.
